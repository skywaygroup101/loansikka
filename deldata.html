Account & Data Deletion
Last updated 2026-09-29

You can delete your LoanSikka account and the personal data associated with it at any time, directly from inside the app. This page explains exactly how to do it, what happens afterwards, and how to reach us if you cannot complete the steps yourself.

If you would rather not follow the in-app steps, you can also send a deletion request by email — see Delete your data by email below.

Delete your account in the app

Four steps, takes less than a minute

Before you start: make sure you are logged in to the Application. The Me tab and the Clear Data option are only available while you are signed in.

1

Open the Me tab

On the main screen, tap the third tab at the bottom right of the screen — labelled Me. It sits next to Home and History.

2

Tap Settings

On the Me page, tap Settings to open the settings screen.

3

Tap Clear Data

In Settings, tap Clear Data. A confirmation dialog will appear asking whether you are sure you want to delete your account and clear all your account data.

4

Tap Confirm

In the dialog, tap Confirm to complete the deletion. Tap Cancel instead if you have changed your mind — nothing will be deleted.

⚠️

Important

Please note that if you have an active loan order, deleting your account may not succeed. Loan records must be retained while an order is outstanding, so the deletion request will be rejected and your account will remain active. Settle your outstanding order first, then try again.

What happens after you confirm

Once the deletion succeeds, the Application signs you out, clears the account data stored on your device, shows an Account Deleted confirmation, and returns you to the login screen.

If the request does not succeed, you will see a failure message and you will stay on the Settings screen — your account will not have been deleted, and you can try again later.

What is deleted, what is kept

Deletion is processed within the timeframes required by applicable law

Deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • The personal and financial profile you submitted during assessment
  • The PAN image you uploaded and the name and PAN number you entered
  • The emergency contacts you provided
  • Financial SMS records you authorised and uploaded
  • Device and technical data linked to your account
  • All account data cached on your device

Kept only where the law requires it

  • Records that must be retained to comply with legal, tax, accounting or anti-money-laundering obligations
  • Records relating to an outstanding or settled loan order, where retention is required by applicable law
  • Aggregated and anonymised data, which no longer identifies you

Delete your data by email

If you cannot access the Application, or you want to delete only some of your personal data rather than your whole account, you can send a request by email. Include the mobile number registered with your LoanSikka account, and tell us what you would like deleted.

The Service Provider will verify your identity before processing the request, and will complete it within the timeframes required by applicable law.
